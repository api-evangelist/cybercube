Dochterman Insurance, the only U.S. brokerage that exclusively serves manufacturers, has launched a fully integrated cyber risk program that combines CyberCube's cyber risk analytics platform with IT assessment and CMMC 2.0 compliance services from Resource Computer Solutions (RCS). The program gives manufacturers a single point of contact for cyber risk identification, IT remediation, federal compliance certification, and insurance placement capabilities that until now required coordinating multiple unrelated vendors.
